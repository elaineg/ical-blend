## Did I see myself in the hero this round?

> "Marketers and ops teams use it to merge client project, webinar, and launch calendars

That's literally my job. The words "marketers", "webinar", "launch calendars", and "for the
whole team" are exactly the language I'd use. First scroll, no clicking — I saw my use case.

## Round 3 → Round 4 movement

## Did it work?
Yes, cleanly. Added US + Canada holiday feeds, opened Options on feed 1, set prefix
`[Webinar]`, ticked "Mask this feed's titles." Hit Create feed → "65 events from 2 sources…
1 feed labelled. 1 feed masked." Got an https Feed URL, a webcal:// URL, AND an "Add to
Google Calendar" button. Copy button put the URL on my clipboard (verified). Pulled the live
feed: masked feed shows `[Webinar] Busy`, the other feed stays detailed. Per-feed prefix +
per-feed mask both actually work. That's the exact thing I wanted — hand the team a feed
where my private launch titles are hidden but the public webinar dates are visible. Phone-
shareable: the webcal:// link is the standard "subscribe on iPhone" path, so yes.

## Honest take / remaining gripe
The hero line is bolted onto the END of a paragraph that still leads with "work, personal,
and shared." It reads like an afterthought, not the headline. The H1 is still "Stop checking
three calendars" — personal-feeling. If I'm forwarding this to my team channel I want the
TOP line to be about teams/feeds, not "three calendars." Also nothing here speaks to the
recurring marketer pain: my webinar/HubSpot dates CHANGE constantly, and I'm trusting "the
live feed auto-refreshes" on faith — I'd want one line of reassurance about how fresh it is.
Minor: the feed URL is a giant opaque blob; fine, but a marketer screenshotting it for the
team will wonder if it's safe to paste.
