# Round 5 — Dana (demand-gen marketer)

## Round 4 → Round 5 movement
Round 4 I was a **gating blocker**. My one complaint: the team/marketer use case was
appended as an afterthought, not co-equal in the H1/lead — I didn't see myself as a
first-class user. **Round 5: that is fixed. I move from gating blocker → not blocking
(soft-positive).**

## Did the fix land?
**Yes.** New H1: "One feed from all your calendars — work, personal, or team" — "team" is
right there, co-equal, no longer buried. Lead now gives equal billing: "merging work,
personal, and family calendars, OR combining client projects, webinar schedules, and
launch timelines for your whole team." That sentence is literally my use case. I see
myself now. The trailing privacy line ("Hide private titles from shared versions with one
checkbox") reads like a marketer-friendly benefit too.

## Flow exercise (mobile 375px, real feeds)
Pasted US + Canada Holidays. Set prefixes "[Webinars]" / "[Campaigns]" via per-feed
Options, masked the second feed. Hit Create feed.
- Result: "65 events from 2 sources... 2 feeds labelled. 1 feed masked." Accurate.
- Preview list rendered with my prefixes actually applied to event titles — not just a
  count, I can SEE it worked.
- Got both a https Feed URL and a webcal:// link + Google/Apple subscribe steps. This is
  the phone-subscribe payoff I came for.
- Zero JS errors through the whole flow.

## Answers
(a) **Advocacy: 8/10.** I'd screenshot this for the team channel — free, no account,
merges feeds, subscribable on my phone, and now it speaks to marketers. Not a 9 because
the privacy disclosure ("URLs... do transit the server") gives me a half-second pause
before I'd put a HubSpot campaign feed URL into a stranger's server, and the feed URL is
a giant opaque string that's mildly intimidating to hand a teammate. Neither is a defect.
(b) **Value clear in under 30s? Yes.** One scroll told me what it does, who it's for, and
that it's free/no-account.
(c) **Biggest remaining item: the "URLs transit the server" trust note + the wall-of-
characters feed URL.** Classification: **soft preference, not a defect.** The app is
honest and the flow works; I just personally hesitate to route a work feed through a
server I don't know. Not gating.
