# Dana — Demand-gen marketer (round 1)

Tested cold on MacBook (1280px), peeked at mobile too. Timed myself.

## Clarity — Yes
Headline "Stop checking three calendars" + subhead "Paste 2–5 calendar links. Get one
feed. No account." told me the whole story in about 8 seconds, one scroll, no marketing
fluff. I'd tell a friend: "It mashes 2–5 calendar URLs into one link you subscribe to once
— free, no login." The "No account" and "Nothing is stored on the server" lines are
exactly what makes me trust it enough to paste a feed. The whole tool — inputs, filters,
Create button — fits above the fold. That's rare and I respect it.

## Value — Marginal (for ME specifically), Yes for the generic merge job
TODAY: my webinar/event feed lives in one place and HubSpot campaign-milestone exports are
a separate ICS. I manually subscribe to both in Google Calendar and they clutter each
other. This genuinely merges them into ONE subscribable link — that part works. I added US
Holidays + Canada Holidays, expanded "Options" on feed 1, typed `[Webinars] `, hit Create:
59 events merged, and the live feed prefixed ONLY the 27 first-feed events with
"[Webinars] " — the Canada events stayed clean. Per-feed scoping is real, not theater.
Why only Marginal: the value over "just subscribe to both calendars separately" is the
prefix + masking, and for my workflow that's a nice-to-have, not a time-saver I'd feel
weekly. I don't re-merge feeds often — I set it once.

## The per-feed Options (the thing I was told to hammer)
- COULD I FIND IT COLD? Barely. "Options" is tiny grey text under each feed row with a
  collapsed ▸ triangle. On cold load I'd have ignored it — it reads like a throwaway link,
  not "this is where the power is." Recommend making it look like a real control.
- PREFIX vs GLOBAL MASK distinction: actually clear, and the copy does the work. The global
  mask says "Applies to all feeds. Need it for just one? Use a feed's Options." — that one
  line is what connected the two for me. Good.
- One nit: the field is labelled "Label added to this feed's event titles" with placeholder
  "[Work]". I typed it expecting a "prefix"; the word "prefix" never appears. Minor, the
  placeholder makes intent obvious.

## Copy cue — Works
Clicked "Copy" on the feed URL → label flipped to "Copied!" with a "Copied to clipboard"
line, and clipboard actually held the URL. Fires correctly.

## Answers
(a) Advocacy: 6/10. It's clean, fast, does what it says, and "free + no signup" is a real
screenshot-for-the-team-channel moment. What holds it back from a 8+: this is a set-once
utility, not a weekly habit for me, so I wouldn't bring it up unprompted often; and the
per-feed Options — the genuinely cool feature — is hidden behind grey 11px text most people
will never expand.
(b) Value clear in <30s? YES. Easily.
(c) Single biggest blocker: the per-feed "Options" disclosure is too easy to miss on cold
load. The differentiator is invisible. Make it look clickable (a button/chip, not grey
text) so people discover prefix/per-feed-mask without being told.
